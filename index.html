Tank Battle Arena — Free 2D Tank Battle Game

Tank Battle Arena is a fast-paced 2D top-down tank battle game inspired by Wii Play. Navigate maze-like arenas, eliminate enemy tanks, and compete across multiple game modes.

Game Modes

  • Solo Campaign — Progress through procedurally generated levels with increasing enemy difficulty. Beat your personal best score across 60+ levels.
  • Arcade Wave Survival — Fight waves of AI enemies powered by Q-learning machine learning. Each wave gets harder as enemies adapt to your tactics.
  • VS AI — Best-of-9 match against a self-learning adaptive opponent that studies your playstyle across rounds.
  • Multiplayer — Real-time online tank battles with friends via Firebase. Create or join lobbies with room codes.

Features

  • 4 tiers of enemy AI with increasing difficulty
  • 5 weapon types: standard, rapid-fire, cannon, shotgun, sniper
  • Deployable mines with 120px blast radius
  • Procedurally generated maze arenas
  • Upgrade system: speed, fuel capacity, mine radius
  • Online leaderboard with Firebase integration
  • Guest sign-in or email authentication
  • Friend codes and invitation system

Controls

WASD to move, mouse to aim, left-click to fire, C to place mines, Escape to pause. Free browser game with no download required — works on desktop and mobile browsers.

📖 GAME GUIDE

Everything you need to know about Tank Battle Arena

🏠 HOME SCREEN

Main buttons: SOLO (campaign), VS AI (practice), VERSUS (online multiplayer).
Bottom bar: Missions, Shop, Upgrades, Loadout, Friends, Season Pass.
Top bar: Your avatar, rank, currencies (gems/coins), sidebar menu ☰.
Sidebar: Full navigation including Settings, Tutorial, Stats, and more.

🎯 GAME MODES

SOLO Campaign: Progress through procedurally generated levels. 4 tiers of enemy AI. Earn scores and stars.
VS AI (Arcade): Best-of-9 adaptive AI that learns your playstyle. 3 difficulty levels.
VERSUS (Multiplayer): Real-time 1v1, 2v2, or 3v3 via Firebase. Create or join lobbies with room codes.

🎒 LOADOUT

Customize your tank: Weapon (different cannons), Skin (visual appearance), Title (displayed name), Gadget (special abilities), Trail (movement effects), Kill Effect (elimination effects). Items are unlocked via the Shop or Season Pass.

🔧 UPGRADES & STAGES

Spend Upgrade Points to improve Speed, Fuel Capacity, and Mine Radius (max level 10 each). When ALL three are maxed, you can Stage Up — resets upgrades to 0 but grants a permanent ×1.12 stat multiplier per stage. This allows endless progression!

🛒 SHOP

Buy weapons, skins, gadgets, trails, and kill effects using Coins or Gems. Items you own can be equipped from the Loadout page.

🔥 SEASON PASS

30 free tiers of exclusive rewards: skins, weapons, titles, coins, and gems. Earn XP from all game modes (10% of match XP goes toward season progress). Claim rewards individually or use CLAIM ALL. New seasons bring new exclusive content.
