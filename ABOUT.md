# Tank Battle Arena

A fast-paced 2D top-down tank battle game inspired by Wii Play. Navigate maze-like arenas, eliminate enemy tanks, and prove your strategic prowess in both single-player and real-time multiplayer modes.

## Purpose

The game recreates the classic Wii Play tank battle experience as a modern web-based application. Players control a tank in procedurally generated maze arenas, aiming and firing at enemy tanks while using walls for cover. Features include:

- **Single Player Campaign**: Progressive difficulty across multiple levels with 4 tiers of enemy AI
- **Local Multiplayer**: Planned 1v1, 2v2, and 3v3 modes
- **Online Multiplayer**: Real-time 1v1 battles via Firebase Realtime Database
- **Procedural Level Generation**: Unique maze layouts each playthrough
- **Destructible Environment**: Mines that destroy walls and damage tanks
- **Scoring System**: Time-based bonuses and kill rewards
